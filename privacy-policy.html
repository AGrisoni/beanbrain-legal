1. Who We Are

BeanBrain ("the App", "BeanBrain", "we", "us", "our") is a spaced-repetition revision application for iPhone, developed and operated by Anthony Grisoni, an individual based in the United Kingdom ("the Developer"). The App is distributed through the Apple App Store.

2. The Short Version

BeanBrain has no account and no BeanBrain server. Your study material and history are stored on your device. The Developer does not receive, store, sell or share your study data. BeanBrain contains no advertising, no third-party analytics or tracking software development kits, and does not ask to track you across other apps or websites.

This policy explains what that means in detail and sets out your rights under applicable law. Where a statement below depends on the version of the App you are using, it describes the App as audited at the date at the top of this policy.

3. Scope and Legal Framework

This policy covers the BeanBrain iOS app and the static legal pages that host this document. The Developer aims to comply with applicable data-protection and privacy law, including the UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR) and the Data Protection Act 2018, the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) where it applies, the California Consumer Privacy Act as amended by the California Privacy Rights Act (CCPA/CPRA), other applicable United States state privacy laws, and Apple's App Store privacy requirements.

Because BeanBrain does not collect or process personal data on any server, and study data stays on your device under your control, the obligations these laws place on the Developer as a controller or business are limited. This policy documents that position.

4. Information Stored on Your Device

BeanBrain stores the study material and history you create on your device, using Apple's on-device storage framework (SwiftData, which is built on Core Data). Depending on how you use the App, this can include:

  • subjects, decks, cards (including prompts, answers, cloze text, comparison and application content), tags and card provenance or source notes;
  • review schedules and scheduling state, including due dates and the difficulty and stability values the scheduling algorithm maintains;
  • review history, including which card was reviewed, when, the rating you gave, and whether you used a hint;
  • study sessions and effective-eligibility history;
  • backlog information, which is derived from your review history and card states;
  • interleaving sets you configure;
  • mock exam questions and options you author, mock exam definitions, exam attempts (including per-question snapshots taken when an attempt began), your selected answers, and exam results;
  • study analytics that BeanBrain computes from the above, such as streaks, a review heatmap, workload forecasts and retention-calibration figures;
  • your notification preferences, such as whether reminders are on, your preferred reminder time, chosen days, a minimum-due threshold and quiet hours.

This data can contain whatever information you choose to type into your cards, questions and notes. It is created and held only on your device. It is not transmitted to the Developer or to any BeanBrain server, because there is no BeanBrain server.

5. Information We Do Not Collect

BeanBrain does not collect, and the Developer does not receive:

  • your name, email address or other contact details;
  • account credentials, passwords or login identifiers (there is no account);
  • location data;
  • device identifiers for advertising or tracking, such as the Identifier for Advertisers;
  • IP address, network information or web-request logs generated by the native app;
  • usage analytics, session recordings or behavioural data;
  • purchase or payment information;
  • camera, microphone, photo library, contacts or calendar data;
  • cookies, pixels, beacons or similar web-tracking technologies (the App is a native iOS application and uses none).

6. Developer Access

The Developer cannot ordinarily view, retrieve, monitor, profile, analyse, sell or share your BeanBrain study data. It is on your device. The Developer has no server database of user information and no mechanism to reach the data on your device.

If you choose to contact the Developer, for example to report a bug, any information you include in that message is processed only to respond to you. Please do not send confidential study material in a support message.

7. The FSRS Scheduling Component

BeanBrain's review scheduling uses swift-fsrs, an open-source implementation of the Free Spaced Repetition Scheduler algorithm by Ben Smiley, used under the MIT Licence. This component runs entirely on your device as part of the App. It performs scheduling calculations locally. It does not connect to the internet, does not transmit your data anywhere, and is not a remote service. Its licence text is available within the App under Settings, then Legal, then Open Source Licences.

8. Local Notifications

If you turn review reminders on, BeanBrain:

  • stores your reminder preferences on your device only;
  • asks iOS for permission to send notifications at the point you enable the feature, and not before;
  • schedules reminders locally using Apple's UserNotifications framework, based on your review workload.

There is no BeanBrain push server, and no third party is involved in delivering these reminders. BeanBrain does not record or analyse whether a notification was delivered, shown or opened.

9. Apple Services and Device Backup

Downloading and using BeanBrain requires an Apple Account and is subject to Apple's terms and privacy policy. Apple may independently process information connected with the App Store download or purchase, device services, notification infrastructure and device or iCloud backup, under Apple's own policies. The Developer has no access to that information and does not receive it.

If you have iCloud Backup or a local device backup enabled, your BeanBrain app data may be included in that backup, in the same way as other apps' data. This is controlled by Apple and by your device settings. BeanBrain does not use CloudKit or any iCloud sync, and does not need to for an ordinary device backup to include its data. The Developer has no access to your backups.

10. The Legal Pages on GitHub

These legal documents are published as static web pages using GitHub Pages, at https://agrisoni.github.io/beanbrain-legal/. The BeanBrain app itself does not send any study data to GitHub.

When you choose to open the Terms of Use or this Privacy Policy from within the App, or visit these pages directly, your browser makes an ordinary web request to GitHub's servers. As part of serving a web page, GitHub may process standard request information such as your IP address, user-agent string and the page requested, under GitHub's own privacy statement. The Developer does not add analytics, tracking, cookies or scripts to these pages, and does not use them to build any profile of you. Opening a hosted web page is not a zero-data event, but no BeanBrain analytics or tracking is involved.

11. External Links

The App or these documents may contain links to external websites, for example a source reference you added to a card, or GitHub's privacy statement above. Those websites are operated independently under their own terms and privacy practices. Once you choose to open an external website, its own practices apply, and the Developer is not responsible for them.

12. Data Retention and Deletion

The Developer retains no server-side study data, because none is collected. Your BeanBrain data stays on your device until you delete it within the App or remove the App. Deleting the App from your device normally removes its local app-container data, subject to any backup you have kept through Apple. You can also delete individual subjects, decks, cards, tags, mock exams, questions, exam attempts and interleaving sets within the App.

13. Security

Because your BeanBrain data stays on your device and is not transmitted to the Developer, its security depends mainly on your device: your passcode, Face ID or Touch ID, and Apple's on-device encryption. Keep your operating system up to date and use device-level security. No method of storage is completely secure, and the Developer cannot guarantee absolute security.

14. Children's Privacy

BeanBrain is not directed at children under 13, and the Developer does not knowingly make it available to them. Because BeanBrain collects no personal data on any server from any user, there is no server-side collection of children's data to address. If you are a parent or guardian and believe a child has used the App, be aware that no personal data has been collected or transmitted by the Developer; you can remove all App data by deleting the App from the device. The Developer does not sell or share the personal data of any person, including anyone under 16.

15. Tracking, Advertising and Universal Opt-Out Signals

The BeanBrain app performs no tracking and shows no advertising. It contains no advertising software development kit, no analytics software development kit, no behavioural analytics and no crash-reporting service, and it does not present an App Tracking Transparency prompt because it does not track you.

Because the native app does no tracking, there is nothing in the app for a "Do Not Track" signal or a Global Privacy Control signal to switch off. Those signals relate to web browsing; if you visit the legal pages in a browser, that browser and GitHub handle any such signals according to their own practices, which is separate from the app.
