8. Learning Algorithms, Scheduling and Estimates

BeanBrain schedules reviews using an implementation of the Free Spaced Repetition Scheduler (FSRS) algorithm, together with values you provide when you rate each review. Using this, BeanBrain calculates and displays quantities such as predicted retrievability, difficulty and stability parameters, due dates, workload forecasts, a review heatmap, retention-calibration charts and other study analytics.

These quantities are computational estimates and organisational tools. They are not measurements of your knowledge, and they are not guarantees. They are affected by how you rate your own recall, how regularly you study, changes you make to your cards, and other use behaviour. A prediction that you are likely to recall a card, a due date in the future, or a favourable analytics trend, is not evidence that you have learned the underlying material or that you will recall it in an examination or in practice.

BeanBrain may change how these calculations work, or the values it uses, in future versions.

9. Self-Assessed Retrieval

When you review a card, you choose a rating, for example "Again", "Hard", "Good" or "Easy". These ratings are your own subjective assessment of how well you recalled the answer. BeanBrain does not independently check whether what you recalled was actually correct, complete or up to date. The scheduling BeanBrain then applies is only as reliable as the ratings you give it.

10. Mock Exams

BeanBrain lets you build and sit multiple-choice mock exams from your own decks, tags or cards. You acknowledge and agree that:

  • mock exams are practice and self-testing tools, not official examinations;
  • a mock exam score is not an official result and does not predict or guarantee your performance in any external examination;
  • a mock exam question pool reflects only the cards and questions you or a provider of study content have put into it, and may not match the current content, format, weighting, marking or standard of any real examination;
  • a mock exam may be sat untimed, or timed with a limit you choose. A timed mock exam runs on wall-clock time and continues while the App is in the background, locked or closed. When the time limit is reached the attempt is submitted automatically;
  • for a timed mock exam that is submitted automatically because time ran out, any question you had not answered at that moment is scored as incorrect for that attempt. The results screen shows how many of the incorrect items were unanswered when time expired, so this is distinguished from questions you answered wrongly;
  • mock exam performance is kept separate from spaced-repetition review scheduling and does not change your review schedule.
